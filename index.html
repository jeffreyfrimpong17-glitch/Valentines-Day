Chapter One

Let Me Tell You Something

My Love,

As Valentine's Day finds us, I find myself overwhelmed with gratitude for you. You didn't just walk into my life; you restructured the whole foundation. Made me want to be better. Made me actually be better.

Times have been rough. We both know that. But through every storm, every late night, every moment of doubt—there you were. You didn't just stand by me. You carried us both.

While you've been out here changing the world—one policy at a time, one case at a time—I've been watching you become the most incredible version of yourself. And it inspires me every single day.

You're brilliant. You're driven. You're absolutely unstoppable. But more than all of that—you're mine. And I'm yours.

This letter? It's just the beginning. Keep scrolling, baby. I've got a lot more to say.

Chapter Two

The Things I Never Said Out Loud

I.

You Changed the Rules

Before you, I had a whole playbook. I knew how things went—how long to wait to text back, how much to care without showing it, how to keep my guard up while pretending I didn't have one.

Then you walked in and rewrote the whole script.

"She didn't break down my walls. She made me realize I didn't need them."

You made vulnerability feel safe. You made effort feel natural. You made me stop calculating and just… love. No strategy. No playbook. Just me, choosing you, every single time.

II.

Through the Storm

I won't pretend this year was easy. There were nights I questioned everything—not you, never you—but myself. Whether I was enough. Whether I was giving you what you deserved.

But every time the weight got heavy, I'd look over and you were still there. Not with judgment. Not with disappointment. Just with patience. Just with love.

"We didn't survive the storm. We danced in it."

That's what I want you to know. I noticed every moment you chose us over easy. Every time you held on when letting go would've been simpler. I saw it all. And I'll spend my life making sure you never regret it.

III.

I See You

I see the woman staying up past midnight finishing case analyses. The one who wakes up and immediately starts grinding because mediocrity was never in your vocabulary.

I see you juggling your master's program and a full-time role at one of the most prestigious firms in the world—and making it look effortless. But I know the effort. I see the hours. I see the sacrifice.

"You don't just work hard. You work like you're building an empire. And baby, the empire looks beautiful from here."

Andrea, I need you to hear this: you are the most remarkable person I've ever met. Not because of your resume. Not because of your ambition. Because of the heart behind all of it. Because you do it all with grace.

IV.

What I Promise You

I can't promise you perfection. I've never been that guy and I never will be. But here's what I can promise:

I will always show up. Not the version of me that's trying to impress you—the real one. The one that's still learning. The one that makes mistakes but never stops trying to be better for you. For us.

"You're my 3 AM thought and my first morning smile. You're the reason I believe in this. All of this."

This? What we have? It's just the beginning. And I intend to spend every chapter after this proving to you that choosing me was the best decision you ever made.

Chapter Three

Our Story, Our Moments

💒

The Wedding

My boy's wedding. I wasn't looking for anything—just there to celebrate. Then I saw you. And suddenly the whole room got quieter.

"The moment I knew? It wasn't dramatic. It was quiet, certain, inevitable."

🏠

Your Mom's House

Your room became our world. Hours turning into late nights, late nights turning into "I should probably go" but neither of us moving. That's where I fell for you—right there in that space that was just ours.

"I kept finding reasons to stay. You were all of them."

🔑

Your First Place

Summer 2025. Watching you walk into your own apartment for the first time—keys in hand, future in your eyes. I was so proud of you that day. Still am.

"Watching you build your life made me want to build one with you."

🇬🇭

Ghana, December 2025

Taking you home. Watching you fall in love with the food, the energy, the people. Seeing you experience my roots—and seeing how naturally you fit into every part of it.

"You didn't just visit my world. You became part of it."

💫

The Little Things

Late night drives. Random grocery runs that turned into adventures. The way you steal my hoodies and pretend you bought them. FaceTime calls that go way too long. Those are my favorite memories—the ones nobody else gets to see.

"It's not the big moments that define us. It's the quiet ones in between."

♾️

Us. Now.

This is the chapter we're writing together. New jobs, new dreams, new cities—whatever comes next, I want it with you. All of it. Every beautiful, messy, uncertain piece.

"The best part of our story? It hasn't even started yet."
